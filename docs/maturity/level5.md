# Level 5 — Are We Getting Faster or Slower?

**Maturity:** Track percentiles over time

Percentiles are plotted as trends, and more than one of them at once — P50,
P75 and P99 together — so that direction becomes visible along with which
releases moved it. Watching several bands rather than a single line is the
whole point: they can move independently, and a headline percentile can hold
perfectly flat while the slowest users get dramatically worse or while gains at
one end quietly cancel losses at the other. Day-to-day variation shows up here
too, which sets a floor on the size of an improvement that can be detected at
all. What the trend still cannot show is the shape of the population between
and beyond those bands.
