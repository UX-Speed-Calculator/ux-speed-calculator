# Level 7 — What Is This Change Worth?

**Maturity:** Compare distributions; correlate to business metrics

Two full distributions are evaluated side by side — before and after, A and B,
us and a competitor, one segment against another. The talk uses three ways of
reading the comparison: overlapping histograms, which show where the two
populations diverge; the cumulative distribution function, which answers what
share of users finished within any given time; and the Q-Q plot, which compares
the two distributions quantile by quantile. Correlating the result to revenue
is what makes a proposed change priceable in advance and verifiable afterwards,
rather than argued in principle. It also closes the ladder: the question is the
one Level 1 asked, now answered with speed as an explicit term rather than an
invisible one. The honest limit is that the relationship between speed and
outcome remains a model calibrated from your own data, not a law of nature.
