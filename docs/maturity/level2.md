# Level 2 — Is My Site Fast or Slow?

**Maturity:** UX speed impacts business

Speed enters the business formula for the first time, as an adjective rather
than a measurement. Two conversion rates sit side by side, labelled fast site
and slow site, with the revenue from Level 1 recomputed under each so the gap
between them reads in money. Belief at this level is carried by other people's
case studies — the talk cites ALDO, where fast rendering brought over four
times the mobile revenue of slow rendering; Ray-Ban, Oakley and Sunglass Hut,
where cost-per-click moves inversely with LCP along `ΔCPC = k × ΔLCP + q`; and
Swappie, where a 23% load time improvement accompanied a 42% increase in mobile
revenue. Borrowed evidence is how the idea enters an organization. What the
level cannot supply is magnitude: fast and slow have no units, so nothing here
can be turned into a target.
