# Level 4 — Fast for Whom?

**Maturity:** Understand that numbers represent users; use percentiles

The number turns out to represent people. Saying 3.4 seconds at the 75th
percentile makes a claim the previous level could not: three quarters of users
had an experience at least this good, and one in four had it worse. P75 is a
convention — the one Core Web Vitals settled on — rather than a law, and
picking a percentile is really a decision about whom you are willing to fail.
Two sites reporting the same duration can be very different once that label is
attached. What is still missing is any sense of movement: the number is a
snapshot, with nothing to say about whether it is better or worse than last
month.
