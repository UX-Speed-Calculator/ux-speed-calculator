# Level 1 — What Matters to My Business?

**Maturity:** Web performance is not understood by business

The starting point is the business itself, with no mention of speed. Total
traffic, conversion rate, order size — a million users, four percent of them
converting, twenty-five dollars an order — that is a million dollars, and that
is the number everything else on this ladder eventually has to move. Conversion
rate is treated here as a single given figure, even though it has many origins:
performance, presentation quality, SEO, availability, and more. At this level
those origins are invisible and unattributed, which is precisely why speed work
is hard to fund from here.
