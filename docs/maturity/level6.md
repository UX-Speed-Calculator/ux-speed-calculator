# Level 6 — What Do All My Users Experience?

**Maturity:** Understand all your users; visualize distributions

The whole population comes into view at once as a distribution of experiences,
rather than being summarized into a point or a handful of tracked bands. The
percentiles from the previous level are still there, but they are landmarks on
something larger now instead of the thing itself. Shape becomes readable: how
wide the spread is, and whether there are separate groups — cached desktop
visits against cold mobile ones, say — that no single number could have
revealed. This is also where the argument stops being statistical and turns
human, because a distribution is a picture of people rather than a summary of
them, and the tail stops being a term of art and becomes the users standing in
it. What the level still cannot supply is a counterfactual: seeing where your
users are says nothing about what moving them would be worth.
